# Cham.ai PayPal AI Hackathon Demo Script
## Total duration: ~180 seconds (3 minutes)

---

## 0:00-0:15 Hook - Problem Statement

**What to show on screen:**
- Quick cut: Traditional chamas image (cash handover, paper records)
- Text overlay: "Traditional chamas: No transparency, no AI insights, slow settlement"
- Quick flashes of modern mobile phones, PayPal logo, AI chat interfaces

**What to say:**
"Traditional chamas rely on trust, spreadsheets, and manual handoffs. No transparency. No AI insights. And slow, risky settlement. What if your savings circle could be transparent, AI-powered, and settle instantly?"

---

## 0:15-0:45 Demo Part 1: Community Creation

**What to show on screen:**
- Login screen → Dashboard
- "Create New Cham" form
- Adding members (names/avatars)
- Setting contribution cycle (weekly/monthly)
- Progress bar showing circle formation

**What to say:**
"First, sign up and create your savings circle. Add members in seconds. Set your contribution cycle—weekly, monthly, whatever your community needs. The dashboard auto-generates the structure, so you spend less organizing and more saving."

---

## 0:45-1:15 Demo Part 2: AI Financial Analysis

**What to show on screen:**
- AI Copilot icon click
- Chat interface: "What's our total contributions this month?"
- Agent Council visualization: Trust Agent → Compliance Agent → Treasury Agent
- Agents "deliberating" with quick thought bubbles
- Final consensus verdict displayed: "Consensus: $4,500 total, 95% compliance"

**What to say:**
"Ask the AI Financial Copilot anything. Behind the scenes, a Council of Agents—Trust, Compliance, Treasury—deliberates in real-time. They verify, validate, and reach consensus. Get instant, trustworthy financial answers without digging through spreadsheets."

---

## 1:15-1:45 Demo Part 3: PayPal Distribution

**What to show on screen:**
- "Distribute Profits" button
- Select members checkboxes
- Amount per member input
- PayPal Payout execution
- Loading spinner → Success confirmation
- PYUSD badge: "Paid with PYUSD on Arbitrum"

**What to say:**
"Approve the payout, select recipients, set amounts per member. PayPal Payouts execute instantly. And yes—it's PYUSD on Arbitrum, so settlements are low-cost, instant, and borderless. No waiting for checks. No bank delays."

---

## 1:45-2:15 Demo Part 4: Gig Escrow

**What to show on screen:**
- "Create Milestone" form
- Worker selection
- Funding escrow (amount + PYUSD badge)
- Milestone completion trigger
- Release payment animation
- Confirmation screen

**What to say:**
"Need to hire a gig worker? Create a milestone, set the worker, fund escrow in PYUSD. When the milestone's done, release payment instantly. Escrow that's transparent, automated, and trustless. The worker gets paid the moment the job's verified."

---

## 2:15-2:30 Closing

**What to show on screen:**
- Cham.ai homepage with AI + PayPal icons
- GitHub repository URL: github.com/chamai/paypal-ai
- Call to action text: "Build trusted collective finance today"

**What to say:**
"AI + PayPal = trusted collective finance. Transparent, AI-powered, instant settlement. Ready to build the future of community finance? Visit our GitHub, fork the repo, and let's make chamas smarter. Thank you."
